What we do

Three practices, one team: the brand expertise of consumer products, the commercial know-how of distribution, and the operational discipline of regulated cross-border trade.

Consulting

Brand development

  • Brand strategy and naming
  • Product and packaging development
  • Private label and manufacturing sourcing
  • Launch and growth planning
Distribution

Nutraceuticals

Distribution of plant-based supplements and nutraceuticals, connecting manufacturers with retailers, practitioners and online channels.

Operations

International import support

Operational support for the import of natural medicines and nutraceuticals: documentation, licensed sourcing, shipping, customs, invoicing and customer support.

  • Regulatory paperwork and authorizations
  • Purchasing from licensed manufacturers
  • International logistics and customs
  • Invoicing, records and customer support

Who we are

Two founders with complementary backgrounds: global consumer brands, and technology-driven operations across the Americas and Europe.

Jill Belasco

Co-founder

Jill is an accomplished entrepreneur and corporate executive whose leadership is widely recognized in the consumer products industry. She was a key player in the fragrance and beauty industry in New York, Paris and Los Angeles.

She served as CEO of the Maesa Group, a French publicly traded company that designs, manufactures and distributes private-label consumer products for retailers and brands around the world, responsible for strategy, growth and innovation across organizations in Paris, New York, Los Angeles and Dongguan, China.

Jill founded Latitudes International Fragrance, established it as an innovative designer and manufacturer of high-quality home fragrance products, and engineered its sale to the Maesa Group in 2007, where she was appointed CEO of the acquiring organization.

Robson Ribeiro

Co-founder

Robson is an experienced business executive and technologist who has worked in multinational and start-up companies in the United States, Italy and Brazil.

His background includes technology companies such as RealNetworks, the inventor of streaming media, where as General Manager for Latin Markets he expanded the company's reach to Latin America and Spain.

Today he leads Meadow's operations and technology, including the platforms that support regulated import operations and customer service for patients and partners in Brazil.

About Meadow

Meadow Holdings is a United States company. We build natural wellness brands, distribute nutraceuticals, and operate the import and support services behind partner platforms that serve patients and consumers in Brazil.

We do not provide medical advice. Where a product requires a prescription or an authorization, every operation we handle starts with the prescription and follows the applicable health and customs regulations.
