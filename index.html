What we do

Importing a prescription medicine for personal use involves several regulated steps. Meadow takes care of them on the patient's behalf, from the first document to the delivery.

1

Regulatory paperwork

Preparation and filing of the documents and authorizations required by Brazilian authorities, in the patient's own name.

2

Sourcing

Purchase of the prescribed product from licensed manufacturers abroad, with batch documentation.

3

Logistics and customs

International shipping with the documentation customs requires, and tracking until delivery.

4

Invoicing and records

Invoices in the patient's name and a complete record of every document, kept available to the patient.

5

Customer support

Answers about the process, document collection and order status, by e-mail and messaging, for patients who contact us.

6

Partner platforms

We operate the import and support services behind patient-facing platforms run by our partners.

About Meadow

Meadow Holdings is a United States company that provides intermediation services for the personal importation of prescription medicines by patients in Brazil. Every import we handle starts with a medical prescription and follows the applicable health and customs regulations.

We do not sell medicines and we do not provide medical advice. Our role is operational: documents, purchase, shipping, invoicing and support.
